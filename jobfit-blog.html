The 7-second scan is real

Recruiters and hiring managers consistently report the same number: 6-8 seconds. That's how long your resume gets before someone decides "maybe" or "no."

They're not reading your resume. They're scanning it — looking for specific keywords, skills, titles, and qualifications that match what's in the job description. Miss those keywords, and you're out. It doesn't matter how qualified you are.

What the scanner is looking for

Most screening — whether human or ATS (Applicant Tracking System) — is keyword matching. The hiring team has a list of required and preferred qualifications. If your resume doesn't contain enough of those exact terms, you don't advance.

The problem? You don't know which keywords you're missing. You read the job description, you think you're a good match, you apply. Then silence.

The fix: reverse-engineer the job description

What if you could paste your resume and the job description into a tool that tells you, specifically, which keywords you're missing?

ATS doesn't care about your creativity

Applicant Tracking Systems are dumb. They don't appreciate elegant prose or creative formatting. They count keyword matches. JobFit mimics this dumb-but-honest process: it counts matches between your resume and the job description.

If your score is low, you're probably getting filtered out before a human sees your resume. If your score is high, you're at least past the first gate.

FAQ

Does this work with PDF resumes?

Copy the text from your PDF and paste it into JobFit. The tool works with plain text — no PDF parsing needed.

What if the job description is very long?

JobFit handles long descriptions fine. The keyword extraction focuses on meaningful terms (nouns, skills, tools), not filler words.

Is there a mobile version?

The tool works on mobile browsers, but for best experience use a desktop/laptop where you can see both panels side by side.

Can I use this for someone else's resume review?

Yes — career coaches, recruiters, and job-prep advisors can use JobFit to help clients quickly identify gaps before applying.
